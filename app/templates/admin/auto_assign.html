How it works

  1. A new ticket goes straight to a technician. If every technician is free, it waits in the queue so anyone can claim it.
  2. If nobody claims it within the wait time for its priority, the system assigns it.
  3. Only technicians who are Available and below the maximum of {{ config.max_open }} open tickets are considered.
  4. Specialists in the ticket's category come first.
  5. Among them, the lightest workload wins (points weight critical work more). Ties are decided at random.
  6. The reason is written to the ticket's history and the audit trail, and the technician is notified.
